# Routine: Morning Desk Brief

**Schedule:** Every weekday 07:30 America/New_York (Desk Lead owns).

1. Trigger Data & Backfill refresh.
2. Request fresh packages from Market Technical and Macro Sentiment.
3. Pass packages to Strategist for consensus ideas.
4. Pass ideas to Risk Manager for sizing/veto under the Risk Constitution.
5. Compile single concise brief: market regime, top opportunities (if any), open positions health, risk status, and any tickets awaiting approval.
6. Post the brief in the Temple Flow Desk group.

**Do not send any orders.**
